# Synthetic sample data

These files are fictional. They were written for the Close & Reconciliation
Assistant portfolio project. Sample Harbor LLC is not a real company. The
files contain no account numbers and no client data.

`data_origin` is `SYNTHETIC` on every row. The loader refuses any other label.

The March 2026 story, on purpose, includes:

- Timing differences: a check and a receipt that clear a few days later, plus a
  deposit in transit and two outstanding payments at month-end.
- A duplicate insurance draft on the bank side.
- A five-dollar amount typo on check CHK-1006. The vendor bill matches the
  book amount.
- A consulting payment with a blank book reference.
- A branch deposit that equals two ledger receipts.
- A bank fee, interest credit, and an unidentified card purchase.
- Expense rows that are clear, vague, conflicting, or duplicated.
