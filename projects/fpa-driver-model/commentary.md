# Variance Commentary

SYNTHETIC DATA — Fairhaven Metrics LLC is a fictional company.

FY 2025 actual revenue was $2,977,650, $222,650 above the original budget of $2,755,000. That beat came from both volume and price. Holding ARPA at the budgeted $2,000, higher average customer volumes would have added $97,000 of revenue. Actual monthly ARPA rose during the year, from $2,000 early on to $2,200 in December, while the budget held ARPA at $2,000 all year; that price increase added the remaining $125,650. The two pieces sum to the $222,650 revenue variance.

Year-end customers landed at 150, seven above the budget’s December ending figure of 143. Actuals added 89 new customers against a budget of 78, and churned 29 against a budget of 25. Net adds were still ahead of plan (60 versus 53).

Total opex came in at $1,431,000, $123,000 above the budget of $1,308,000. A higher opex variance is unfavorable, even though revenue also beat budget. People cost was the main driver: $1,359,000 actual versus $1,248,000 budget, or $111,000 over. Other opex was $72,000 versus $60,000, or $12,000 over.

Operating income was $951,120, $55,120 above the budget of $896,000. The revenue beat more than offset the extra cost. After COGS moved with revenue, gross profit was $178,120 above budget, and after the $123,000 of additional opex, operating income was still ahead by $55,120.

The 2026 forecast, built from the monthly drivers, shows 217 ending customers, December ARPA of $2,300, December headcount of 19, and operating income of $2,068,605, compared with 2025 actual operating income of $951,120. This is a forecast from drivers, not a promise.
