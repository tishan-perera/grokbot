## Conventions

- Customer rollforward: **Ending customers = Beginning customers + New customers − Churned customers**.
- Beginning customers in each month equal ending customers of the prior month. January 2025 begins at 90. **January 2026 beginning customers must equal December 2025 ending customers** — this is a cell reference on the Actuals tab, not a retyped number.
- **Average customers = (Beginning + Ending) / 2**.
- **Revenue = Average customers × monthly ARPA**. This model uses average customers, not ending customers, for revenue. Monthly ARPA is average revenue per account per month, in USD — not annual ARPA.
- **COGS = Revenue × 20%**.
- **People cost** = Engineering headcount × Engineering cost per head, plus the same for Sales, Customer Success, and G&A.
- **Total opex = People cost + Other opex**.
- **Gross profit = Revenue − COGS**.
- **Operating income = Gross profit − Total opex**.
- **ARR** (on the Summary tab) = December ending customers × December monthly ARPA × 12.
- **Variance $ = Actual − Budget**. A positive revenue or operating-income variance is favorable. A positive cost variance is unfavorable. The commentary states that clearly.
- **Variance % = Variance $ / Budget**, when Budget is not zero.

FY totals: flow items can be summed (new and churned customers, revenue, COGS, gross profit, people cost, other opex, total opex, operating income). Beginning or ending customers, average customers, ARPA, headcount, and cost per head are not summed for a year total. For those, the model uses a point-in-time figure (usually December) or a stated average.

## Key company assumptions

All of the following are fictional and generated by `generate_model.py`.

- Company: Fairhaven Metrics LLC (fictional B2B SaaS). Currency: USD. Calendar year.
- 2025 actuals: customers grow from **90** at 1 January to **150** at 31 December (89 new, 29 churned).
- Monthly ARPA rises during 2025 from **$2,000** to **$2,200**.
- 2025 original budget used the same 90 opening customers, a more conservative new-customer plan, no ARPA increase (ARPA stays $2,000), and slower hiring.
- 2026 forecast drivers start from the 150 actual ending customers. New and churned customer counts, ARPA, headcount, 2026 cost-per-head rates, and other opex are typed monthly values on Inputs-Drivers.
- 2026 cost per head is **3% above** the 2025 rates (Engineering $10,300, Sales $8,755, Customer Success $7,210, G&A $8,240 per month, fully loaded).
- Departments: Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, and G&A. 2025 actual headcount moves from 11 in Q1 to 15 in Q4. 2026 drivers continue hiring, ending the year at 19.
- Other opex is a monthly cash operating spend (tools, programs, and similar), not people cost.

The generator script is the source of truth for these input numbers. It writes only the empty template name `Fairhaven_Metrics_FP&A_Model_SYNTHETIC.xlsx` and `test_cases.md`. It does not overwrite the completed workbook `Fairhaven_Metrics_FP&A_Model_SYNTHETIC_built.xlsx`. Re-running the generator would replace `test_cases.md` if that file is in the same folder.

## How to check the completed workbook

From this folder, with Python 3 and `openpyxl` installed:

```bash
python3 verify_model.py "Fairhaven_Metrics_FP&A_Model_SYNTHETIC_built.xlsx"
```

With no argument, `verify_model.py` looks for the empty template filename `Fairhaven_Metrics_FP&A_Model_SYNTHETIC.xlsx`, which is not included in this folder. Pass the built workbook path as shown above.

`generate_model.py` writes only `Fairhaven_Metrics_FP&A_Model_SYNTHETIC.xlsx` (the empty template name) and `test_cases.md`. It will not overwrite `Fairhaven_Metrics_FP&A_Model_SYNTHETIC_built.xlsx`. Do not run it in this folder unless you intend to add an empty template and replace `test_cases.md`.
